How the stages work

Only the 68 per 100’000 epidemic threshold is an official FOPH definition — it is the level at which a seasonal flu epidemic is declared in Switzerland. The bands above and below it are derived from the distribution of weekly national incidence since 2013 (roughly the 90th and 95th percentiles) to give the single threshold some shape. Treat them as a reading aid, not as official policy levels.

Consultations vs confirmed cases

The headline figure counts consultations for flu-like illness extrapolated from the Sentinella network of GPs — a broad activity signal, not confirmed flu. The case counts are laboratory-confirmed influenza reported under the mandatory reporting system; they are a large undercount of real infections, since most people with flu are never tested.

Reading the swab panel

Sentinella GPs send a subset of their flu-like-illness patients’ swabs for PCR, and each swab is tested for influenza, RSV and SARS-CoV-2 together. The three rates therefore share one denominator and can sum above 100%, because a swab can be positive for more than one virus. They say what is circulating among people who saw a doctor and were swabbed — not how common each virus is in the population. The denominator collapses outside flu season, sometimes to fewer than ten swabs a week, and at that point a single detection swings the rate by tens of percentage points; the note under the chart gives the week’s swab count so the thin weeks are visible rather than implied.

Reading the wastewater panel

Everything else here counts people who saw a doctor. Wastewater does not: it measures virus shed into the sewers of eleven treatment plants, so it is unaffected by who feels ill enough to seek care, by testing rates, or by the holidays. It is also daily, and usually a little ahead of the weekly reporting above. The trade-off is coverage — those eleven plants serve about 2 million of Switzerland’s 9 million people, so this is a sample of the country, not the country.

The three panels each carry their own vertical scale, and that is deliberate. How much virus an infected person sheds differs enormously between these viruses, so SARS-CoV-2 load running an order of magnitude above influenza says nothing about how many people have either one. Compare each virus against its own past, never one against another.
